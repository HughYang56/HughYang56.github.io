About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Statistics at the University of California, Riverside. My research focuses on machine learning for structured and dynamic data, with particular interests in graph deep learning, multimodal learning, large language models, transfer learning, and spatio-temporal prediction.

I develop learning methods that incorporate geometric and topological structure and apply them to problems in healthcare, urban dynamics, financial security, and knowledge-driven intelligent systems.

Research interests

  • Graph and topological learning: graph neural networks, graph pre-training, and topology-aware representation learning.
  • Temporal modeling: temporal point processes, diffusion models, and spatio-temporal event prediction.
  • Multimodal and transfer learning: cross-domain learning for healthcare and financial applications.
  • Large language models: efficient LLMs, model compression, and knowledge graph-augmented systems.
